Module 1 Intersection of Line & Parabola

Official SAT Practice Style Question

A system of equations consists of y = x² - 4x + 3 and y = 2x - 2. What is the x-value of the intersection point where x > 2?
Why Manual Math is Tricky

Requires setting equal (x² - 6x + 5 = 0), factoring or quadratic formula, and testing x > 2 restriction under time pressure.

TI-84 Evo Shortcut

Graph both equations in Y=, then use 2nd -> TRACE (CALC) -> Intersect for instant solution with zero manual algebra errors.

Step-by-Step Guide (1/4)

Step 1: Enter Equations

Press [Y=] on the key pad. Input Y1 = X² - 4X + 3 and Y2 = 2X - 2.

SAT non-linear system questions are specifically constructed with traps (like asking only for x when x > 2). Graphing directly removes sign errors and gives you visual proof of all intersection points.
